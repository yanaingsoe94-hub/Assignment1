My Summer Adventures

My Trip to California

It was a long, quick trip. We loaded our vehicle and headed to Northern California for the 4th of july weekend for a family reunion. We then made haste to Magic Mountain. By the time we returned home, we had traveled some 1,300 milesin three and one half days.

My Trip To The Rockies

After a year's preparation, it was time to go backpacking with the Boy Scouts.We flew to Colorado Springs, toured the Air Fore Acadamy, Pike's Peal, Garden Of The Gods,and the Olympic training center. We chartered a bus to the Philmont Scout Ranch in Northern New Mexico and began our 60 mile backpack trek. Afterwards, we boarded Amtrack's and rode the rails to Kingman, and then chartered a bus home.
